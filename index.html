About Us

A Centre Built Around Children and Their Families

Care by Chanchal is an institute for special needs children in Sector 84, Gurugram. Every child who walks through our doors is met first as a person, with their own strengths, interests and pace. From early intervention to everyday life skills, everything we do is built around what helps your child flourish.

Our Approach

  • We listen before we plan.
  • We follow your child's pace, never a formula.
  • We work in partnership with you, the people who know them best.

Our Mission

To nurture every child's potential with patience, confidence and dignity, while walking alongside families at every step of their journey.

Our Services

How We Support Your Child

Every child's needs are different, so every plan is different. All our support is tailored around your child, at a pace that feels right for them.

  • Early Intervention

    Gentle, timely support in the early years, when small steps matter most.

  • Developmental Assessment

    Understanding your child's strengths and needs, so support starts right.

  • Speech & Language Therapy

    Helping children understand, express and connect in their own way.

  • Special Education

    Personalised learning plans that adapt to how your child learns.

  • Behaviour & Social Skills

    Patient guidance for emotions, friendships and everyday situations.

  • Psychological Support

    A safe, supportive space for emotional and behavioural needs.

  • Parent Training

    Practical strategies so you can support your child at home, every day.

  • School Readiness

    Building the confidence and skills for a happy start to school.

  • Vocational & Pre Vocational Services

    Meaningful skill building towards independence and opportunity.

  • AAC & Assistive Support

    Tools and techniques that give every child a voice.

  • Referral & Care Coordination

    Helping families connect with the right professionals and care.

  • Not Sure Where to Begin?

    That's perfectly okay. Share your concerns with us, and we'll figure it out together, gently.

Privacy Policy

At Care by Chanchal, we respect your privacy. This policy explains, in simple words, how this website handles your information.

What we collect

Only the details you choose to share when you send an enquiry: your name, your phone number, your child's age, the support you're looking for, and your message.

How we use it

Solely to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about it. Nothing more.

What we never do

This website has no database and no tracking. Your enquiry is not stored on the website, there are no advertising cookies or analytics trackers, and your information is never sold or shared with anyone.

Questions?
